# Overall Product Vision

The finished technical output is best described as a secure legal document management and tracking platform rather than simply a digital signature system. Its main capabilities are to:

- Store legal documents securely in the cloud
- Track each document through its legal workflow
- Apply REE-compliant digital signatures
- Detect document tampering using SHA-256 hashing
- Protect signing certificates with AES-256 encryption
- Maintain an immutable audit trail (chain of custody)
- Bridge physical case folders and digital records using QR codes
- Verify the authenticity of downloaded documents through hash comparison
